# FAQ and About us content

Reviewed 14 September 2026. The five FAQ topics are editorial priorities for this first-run screen, not a measured ranking of support queries: cost, beginner/language support, speaking partner, real-life usefulness, and daily practice time. Copy avoids labelling users by income, jargon such as “freemium”, or guaranteed fluency/job outcomes.

- Free use with optional paid upgrades: product owner confirmation in this task. The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/speakx-english-speaking-app/id6756059415) also lists Free / In-App Purchases. No unverified feature limits, prices or billing promises are stated.
- Sia, seven supported Indian languages, conversation topics and Progress Report: [official Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=yellowclass.kids.live) and [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/speakx-english-speaking-app/id6756059415).
- About 15 minutes of daily practice: [SpeakX homepage](https://speakx.in/). Presented as a suggested habit, not a guaranteed time to fluency.
- Indian company and mission: [official About page](https://speakx.ai/business/about). Legal developer name and Indian address: Google Play and App Store listings.
- 1 crore+ downloads: Google Play shows 10M+ downloads. This is labelled downloads, not active learners.
- MS Dhoni as an investor: [SpeakX homepage](https://speakx.in/) lists him among angel investors and links to coverage. The About panel describes his role as investor, not teacher.

The pricing answer is expanded by default. The shared support component applies to all three variants. About us links its two factual trust points to the official website and Google Play so users can check them.
